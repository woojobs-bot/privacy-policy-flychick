Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

This app is a stand-alone game that does not collect or transmit any personal data to external servers.

1. Data Collection

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal information. All game progress is saved locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use non-personally identifiable information, such as advertising IDs (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android), to provide personalized ads.

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us via the app store developer contact.
